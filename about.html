WHO ARE WE?

We are The Publishing Department from the Kingdom of Lesotho.
We aim to provide open access to important documents based on biblical truth.
The Publishing department exist to share the gospel of hope and to make ready the people of God in a world fast approaching its end.

WHAT WE DO

Under the denomination of IMSSDARM in Lesotho field, we translate and compile leaflets, booklets and other reading material.
Our materials are prayerfully crafted with the sole aim of pointing hearts to Christ and helping believers grow in their walk with God.
We are honored to work alongside authors, ministers, and all our supporters from across the globe.
